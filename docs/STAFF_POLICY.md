# Folub & Samuel Labs: Staff Policy

**Applies to staff, contractors and Academy instructors.**

_Version 1.0 · 3 August 2026_

---

## How to read this document

The Company Handbook explains who FSLabs is and how work is judged. This document covers the terms
of working with us: hours, leave, pay, what you may and may not do with client material, who owns the
work, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Where this document says a founder approves something, that means Adeseko Samuel for technical and
security matters, and Akinbayo for anything commercial or client facing.

If something here is unclear, ask before assuming. That instruction applies to this document as much
as it does to client work.

---

## 1. Working hours and location

We work remote first. You choose where you work.

You are expected to be reachable and responsive between **10:00 and 16:00 WAT, Monday to Friday**.
Outside that window, arrange your hours to suit your work and your life. Nobody is measuring when you
are online; we are measuring whether the work meets the standard and whether people can reach you
when it matters.

Where a client requires you on site, that is agreed with Akinbayo in advance and treated as part of
the engagement.

If you will be unreachable during core hours, say so beforehand. An unannounced silence during core
hours is the only version of this that becomes a problem.

## 2. Annual leave

Full time staff receive **15 working days of paid annual leave per year**, in addition to Nigerian
public holidays.

- Leave accrues monthly.
- Requests need **two weeks notice** and are approved by Akinbayo.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried into the following year.
- Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Tell a founder as early as you can and do not work while
  unwell.

Approval is normally a formality. It is asked for so that client commitments can be covered, not to
gatekeep your time off.

## 3. How you are paid

**FSLabs does not yet run a fixed monthly payroll.** That is stated plainly because a policy that is
vague about money is worse than one that admits where the company is. There are two ways you are paid
today.

### 3.1 Payment for work you deliver

Before any engagement starts, an amount is agreed **in writing** for the work you are doing. It is
paid within **14 days** of the client settling the related invoice.

Academy instructors are paid per cohort, on completion, on the same 14 day terms.

No work should ever begin without that written figure. If someone asks you to start without one, that
is a mistake to escalate, not a favour to do.

### 3.2 Commission for work you bring in

Anyone who introduces a client or project that FSLabs goes on to win is paid **10% of the total value
of that contract**, in instalments as the client settles each invoice.

This applies whoever you are and whatever your role. If you bring the company work, you share in it.

Commission is calculated on the contract value for FSLabs services, excluding amounts that pass
straight through to a third party, such as cloud spend, hardware or software licences bought on the
client's behalf. Those are not our revenue and are not commissionable.

### 3.3 Salaries

The founders review the company's position **quarterly** and will introduce salaries as revenue
allows. There is no target figure attached to that yet, and none is claimed here.

You are entitled to ask where the company stands at any review point, and entitled to a straight
answer.

### 3.4 Expenses

Work related expenses are reimbursed on production of a receipt. Anything substantial needs Akinbayo's
approval **before** you spend it, not after.

## 4. Client data, security and AI tools

This is the most important section in this document. We sell security. A breach of this section is
not an administrative slip; it is the company failing at the thing it charges people for.

### 4.1 The rule on AI tools

**Client code, client data, credentials, and security findings must never be entered into any third
party AI tool.** Not into a free tier, not into a personal account, not "just to check something".

AI tools are welcome for general work: research, drafting, learning, exploring an unfamiliar library
on a scratch project of your own.

If you are unsure whether something counts as client data, **treat it as client data and ask Samuel.**

Where a client specifically wants AI assisted work on their material, that is a written exception
approved by Samuel, carried out through an FSLabs business account with training data sharing turned
off. The exception is recorded. It is never assumed.

### 4.2 Confidentiality

- Client code, client data, client pricing, and our internal delivery economics stay inside FSLabs.
- **Security findings never leave.** What we discover on a client's systems belongs to that client and
  to us, and to nobody else. Not in a conference talk, not in a portfolio, not as an anonymised
  example, not in a group chat.
- Never name a client publicly, in a deck, on social media, in a portfolio, or in conversation
  outside FSLabs, without confirmed written permission.
- These obligations continue after you stop working with us.

### 4.3 Device and credential security

- Full disk encryption and a screen lock on any device you use for FSLabs or client work.
- Credentials go in the company password manager. Never in code, never in a chat message, never in a
  document, never in a support ticket.
- Multi factor authentication on every account that offers it.
- Report a lost or stolen device to both founders immediately. Immediately means that hour, not that
  week.
- Client production systems are accessed only with the access you were granted, for the purpose you
  were granted it.

## 5. Who owns the work

**Client deliverables.** Ownership of the deliverable transfers to the client on full payment.

**What we keep.** FSLabs retains ownership of anything that existed before an engagement, and of
general purpose components, libraries and tooling we reuse across clients. The client receives a
perpetual licence to use those as part of their deliverable. This is what lets us build on our own
work rather than starting every project from nothing, and it is a standard position that clients
accept.

**Your work.** Staff, contractors and Academy instructors assign to FSLabs all intellectual property
in work they produce for us or for our clients.

**Your own projects.** Anything you build in your own time, on your own equipment, unrelated to
FSLabs work and to our clients, is yours. If the line is unclear on something you are excited about,
ask Samuel before you start rather than after you ship.

## 6. When something goes wrong

This covers a production system failing, a suspected breach in a client system, and a suspected
breach in ours.

**Call both founders immediately, whatever the hour.**

1. **Do not contact the client yourself.** Not to reassure them, not to ask a question, not to
   apologise.
2. **Samuel leads containment and diagnosis.**
3. **Akinbayo is the only person who communicates with the client.** One voice, so no client ever
   hears a half diagnosed problem.
4. **Do not destroy evidence.** Do not wipe logs, reset the machine, or delete anything in an attempt
   to tidy up. Preserve the state.
5. **Write down what you saw and when**, while it is fresh, including what you were doing when it
   started.

Where personal data may have been exposed, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission must be notified
within **72 hours**. That clock is short and it does not wait for certainty, so escalate even when you
are unsure whether it qualifies.

**Nobody has ever been in trouble here for escalating too early.** Raising something that turns out to
be nothing is a good outcome. Sitting on something because you were not certain is the failure.

## 7. Joining and leaving

### On joining you will be set up with

- An FSLabs email account
- Access to the company password manager
- Access to the repositories and systems your role needs, and no more
- Access to the project tooling and any client systems specific to your work
- The Company Handbook and this policy document

Access is granted per role and per project. If you are given access you do not need, say so, and it
will be removed. That is not pedantry; it is the same principle we sell to clients.

### On leaving

- All access is revoked on your last day.
- Company and client devices are returned.
- Accounts, repositories and documents you own are transferred to a founder.
- Your confidentiality and intellectual property obligations continue.

We will always tell you what is expected on your way out, in writing. We ask that you give us the
same clarity.

## 8. What is not settled yet

Honesty about the gaps, rather than pretending this is complete:

- **Salary levels and timing.** Reviewed quarterly, as set out in 3.3.
- **Probation terms and formal performance review.** Not yet defined. For now, feedback is direct and

- **Contractor agreement template.** In progress. Until it exists, terms are agreed in writing per
  engagement, which is the requirement in 3.1 regardless.

When any of these is settled, this document is updated and reissued with a new version number. It is
not changed quietly.
