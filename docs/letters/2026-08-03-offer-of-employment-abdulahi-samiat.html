2. Purpose of the Role

The Content Strategist owns how FSLabs sounds and what FSLabs publishes. You will set the editorial direction across the Company’s brands and products — including the FSLabs corporate brand, FS Exchange, FSLabs Training, and FOLUB Builders — and translate technical work in software engineering and cybersecurity into clear, credible, audience-first content that earns attention, builds trust, and brings in qualified enquiries. You are not a posting assistant. You are the person who decides what story we are telling, to whom, on which platform, and why it will work.

3. Key Responsibilities and Job Roles

3.1  Content Strategy and Planning

  • Own and maintain the Company’s content strategy across all brands, products, and channels, reviewed quarterly against business objectives.
  • Define target audiences and content pillars for each brand, and document the reasoning so the strategy survives handover.
  • Build and run monthly content calendars covering topics, formats, channels, publishing cadence, and ownership.
  • Run periodic content audits, retire what underperforms, and reallocate effort toward what demonstrably works.
  • Advise leadership on positioning, messaging, and launch sequencing for new products, features, and services.

3.2  Writing and Editorial

  • Research, write, and edit long-form and short-form content: articles, explainers, case studies, thought-leadership pieces, newsletters, landing-page copy, product copy, scripts, and campaign copy.
  • Translate technical subject matter — security incidents, engineering decisions, product mechanics — into accessible narrative without diluting accuracy.
  • Interview internal engineers, security staff, and clients to source original material rather than recycling generic industry commentary.
  • Fact-check every published claim, and verify all technical statements with the Chief Technology Officer before release.
  • Serve as final editorial gate on tone, clarity, grammar, and house style for anything published under a Company brand.

3.3  Brand Voice and Storytelling

  • Author and maintain the FSLabs brand voice and editorial style guide, including per-brand variations.
  • Develop the founder and company narrative into a consistent, recognisable public voice across platforms.
  • Build storytelling assets around client outcomes, project milestones, and behind-the-scenes engineering work, subject to client consent and the confidentiality obligations in this letter.
  • Protect brand consistency by reviewing outbound material produced by other functions before publication.

3.4  Social Media and Community Growth

  • Plan, produce, schedule, and publish content across the Company’s social platforms, currently LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and TikTok.
  • Set and defend a consistent publishing cadence, and hold it through quiet periods as well as launch periods.
  • Grow reach, engagement, and qualified follower base through deliberate, tested content formats rather than volume alone.
  • Manage community interaction — comments, mentions, and direct enquiries — escalating commercial leads to the Business Lead and security-sensitive enquiries to the Chief Technology Officer.
  • Monitor platform, industry, and cultural trends, and judge which are worth acting on for a technology and cybersecurity firm.

3.5  SEO and Organic Discoverability

  • Conduct keyword and search-intent research for the Company’s websites and product pages.
  • Structure and optimise on-page content — titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal linking — to improve organic visibility.
  • Maintain a rolling SEO content roadmap targeting the queries our prospective clients actually search.
  • Work with the technical team on content-adjacent SEO matters, including page structure, performance, and metadata.

3.6  Campaign and Commercial Support

  • Develop content-led campaigns tied to defined business outcomes: enquiries, waitlist sign-ups, training enrolments, and platform registrations.
  • Write and maintain email and newsletter sequences for onboarding, nurture, and announcement flows.
  • Produce supporting content for sales and partnership conversations, including one-pagers, decks, and brochure copy.
  • Support the FSLabs Training and FS Exchange product lines with launch, enrolment, and retention content.

3.7  Measurement and Reporting

  • Define the content metrics that matter for each brand and channel, and set baselines in the first month.
  • Track performance and report monthly to the Chief Technology Officer with findings and a clear recommendation, not raw numbers alone.
  • Run structured tests on formats, hooks, and cadence, and fold the results back into strategy.

3.8  Collaboration and Governance

  • Work closely with engineering, security, design, and business functions to source accurate material and align messaging.
  • Brief and review the work of freelancers, designers, or interns engaged to support content production.
  • Comply with the Company’s information-security and acceptable-use policies in all content work, and treat client and system information as confidential by default.
  • Maintain organised, accessible content records so that calendars, assets, and performance history remain Company property and are not lost to personal accounts.
