2. Purpose of the Role

The Content Strategist owns how FSLabs sounds and what FSLabs publishes. You will set the editorial direction across the Company’s brands and products — the FSLabs corporate brand, FS Exchange, FSLabs Training, and FOLUB Builders — and translate technical work in software engineering and cybersecurity into clear, credible, audience-first content that earns attention and brings in qualified enquiries. You are not a posting assistant. You are the person who decides what story we are telling, to whom, and why it will work.

3. Key Responsibilities and Job Roles

3.1  Content Strategy and Planning

  • Own the Company’s content strategy across all brands, products, and channels, reviewed quarterly against business objectives.
  • Define target audiences and content pillars per brand, and build and run the monthly content calendar.
  • Audit content periodically, retire what underperforms, and reallocate effort toward what demonstrably works.
  • Advise leadership on positioning, messaging, and launch sequencing for new products and services.

3.2  Writing, Editorial, and Brand Voice

  • Research, write, and edit across formats: articles, explainers, case studies, newsletters, landing-page and product copy, scripts, and campaign copy.
  • Translate technical subject matter — security incidents, engineering decisions, product mechanics — into accessible narrative without diluting accuracy.
  • Interview internal engineers, security staff, and clients to source original material rather than recycling industry commentary, fact-checking every claim and verifying technical statements with the Chief Technology Officer before release.
  • Author and maintain the FSLabs brand voice and editorial style guide, and serve as final editorial gate on anything published under a Company brand.

3.3  Social Media and Community Growth

  • Plan, produce, schedule, and publish across the Company’s social platforms — currently LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and TikTok — holding a consistent cadence through quiet periods as well as launch periods.
  • Grow reach, engagement, and qualified following through deliberate, tested formats rather than volume alone.
  • Manage community interaction, escalating commercial leads to the Business Lead and security-sensitive enquiries to the Chief Technology Officer.

3.4  SEO and Organic Discoverability

  • Conduct keyword and search-intent research for the Company’s websites and product pages, and maintain a rolling SEO content roadmap.
  • Optimise on-page content — titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal linking — working with the technical team on page structure and metadata.

3.5  Campaigns and Commercial Support

  • Develop content-led campaigns tied to defined outcomes: enquiries, waitlist sign-ups, training enrolments, and platform registrations.
  • Write and maintain email and newsletter sequences for onboarding, nurture, and announcement flows.
  • Support the FSLabs Training and FS Exchange product lines, and produce content for sales and partnership conversations.

3.6  Measurement, Collaboration, and Governance

  • Define the metrics that matter per brand and channel, set baselines in the first month, and report monthly to the Chief Technology Officer with a recommendation, not raw numbers alone.
  • Run structured tests on formats, hooks, and cadence, and fold results back into strategy.
  • Work with engineering, security, design, and business functions to source accurate material and align messaging; brief any freelancers engaged to support production.
  • Treat client and system information as confidential by default, and keep content records organised and accessible as Company property.
