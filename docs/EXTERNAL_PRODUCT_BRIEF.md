## The products

### TailorNow
**tailornow.shop · live · consumer marketplace · Nigeria**

A marketplace for custom fashion. It connects customers with verified tailors and fashion creatives and
runs the whole order end to end: browsing portfolios and a style feed, booking, saved measurements,
agreed pricing, payment with automatic split settlement to the creative, production tracking, fabric
pickup and delivery, and mutual ratings. It also supports group and aso ebi orders and corporate
uniforms.

- **Who it is for.** Two sides. Customers who want clothes made rather than bought, and tailors and
  fashion creatives who want steady orders and to get paid reliably.
- **Business model.** 20% platform commission on the order, plus a 3% service charge.
- **The marketing problem.** A two sided marketplace, so supply and demand have to grow together.
  Creatives are the harder and more valuable side to acquire, and most of them are currently cash based
  and offline.
- **This is our most marketable asset.** Visual category, young audience, strong social potential,
  and a launch story about formalising informal craft businesses.

### FSLabs Exchange
**live · consumer fintech · Nigeria**

Crypto to naira and account services. USDT, gift cards and related requests, processed within about 30
minutes.

- **Who it is for.** People converting crypto and gift card value to naira who care about speed and
  trust.
- **The marketing problem.** A trust category with heavy competition and real advertising restrictions
  on most major platforms. Any plan here needs to be channel realistic.

### HR and Payroll platform
**in final review · B2B SaaS · Nigerian businesses**

Multitenant software for employees, departments, attendance, leave, documents, payroll records, audit
logging and subscription billing.

- **Who it is for.** Nigerian businesses of roughly twenty to two hundred staff moving off spreadsheets
  and WhatsApp.
- **Business model.** Subscription.
- **Status.** Built and working, currently in a review and debug pass before we put it in front of
  paying businesses. Treat it as launching soon rather than available now.
- **The marketing problem.** Longer sales cycle, decision maker is a founder or an HR lead, and the
  competition is inertia rather than another product.

### UrbanPulse
**in development · civic and public sector**

AI assisted citizen reporting and transport intelligence for African cities.

- **Who it is for.** City residents, and the transport authorities, government agencies and development
  funders who need the data.
- **Status.** In active development, not open to users. Included here for context on where the company
  is going. We would want a mutual NDA in place before going into detail on this one.

## What we are looking for

We have not decided the scope yet, which is what we would like your input on. The questions we are
trying to answer:

1. Which of these products deserves marketing spend first, and why.
2. What a realistic launch plan looks like for a two sided marketplace in Nigeria, given a small budget
   and no existing audience.
3. Whether the right first move is paid acquisition, creator and community work, content, or something
   else entirely.
4. What you would need from us to make that work, in time, assets and money.

Tell us what you think we are getting wrong. We would rather hear that now than after a campaign.
